# About Sakar: Connecting People With the Soul of Nepal

For Sakar, Nepal has never been just a destination. It has always been a story waiting to be understood.

Growing up in the Kathmandu Valley, he was surrounded by a living heritage — ancient temples, traditional courtyards, festivals, artisans, and communities where history was not preserved only in books, but experienced through everyday life.

However, Sakar’s connection with Nepal grew beyond its beautiful landscapes and historic places. Through his involvement in education, social development, and community work, he had the opportunity to walk closer with the people of Nepal — listening to their stories, understanding their challenges, and witnessing their resilience.

These experiences shaped the way he sees travel.

For Sakar, a journey is not simply about visiting a monument, capturing a photograph, or following a fixed itinerary. It is about discovering the meaning behind a place — the people, traditions, emotions, and stories that give it life.

* A temple represents generations of faith and devotion.  
* A village reflects the dreams, memories, and relationships of its people.  
* A mountain represents not only nature’s beauty, but also humility, strength, and connection.

This belief inspired him to create **Explore With Sakar** — a platform dedicated to meaningful experiences that connect travellers with the deeper essence of Nepal.

Through his journeys, Sakar introduces Nepal through its hidden corners, local communities, ancient wisdom, Himalayan landscapes, and the relationship between nature, culture, and humanity.

His role goes beyond showing places. As a cultural bridge and local fixer, he helps create authentic connections — bringing together travellers with local communities, artisans, traditions, and experiences that are often missed through ordinary travel.

## Sakar’s Reflections: Stories Beyond the Journey

Every journey begins long before reaching a destination.

For Sakar, the journey has always been about understanding — understanding people, cultures, nature, and the deeper meaning behind human experiences.

From working with communities to exploring Nepal’s heritage, from learning about Himalayan traditions to walking through villages and mountains, each experience has shaped his perspective.

He believes every person carries a story, every place holds memories, and every tradition carries the wisdom of generations.

His connection with Nepal is found in simple moments — a conversation with an elder, the sound of temple bells at sunrise, the craftsmanship of an artisan, the warmth of a village family, or the silence of the Himalayas.

Over time, he discovered that travel can become more than movement between places.

It can become a journey of learning, connection, and self-discovery.

His interest in spirituality, meditation, Himalayan traditions, and ancient wisdom comes from a curiosity to understand the deeper relationship between humans, nature, and the universe. He believes that while cultures may express their wisdom differently, many share a common desire — to find balance, harmony, and connection.
