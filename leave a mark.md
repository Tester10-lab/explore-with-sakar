# Leave a Mark: Strategic Community Impact Journey

## Background & Concept

**Leave a Mark** is an initiative that connects travellers, professionals, and local communities to create meaningful social impact through knowledge exchange, collaboration, and responsible resource mobilisation. The initiative moves beyond conventional volunteering by focusing on strengthening existing community efforts through skills, strategic support, and carefully planned projects.

---

## Project Identification & Community Partnership

Every project is chosen based on authentic community needs and long-term sustainability:

* **Needs Identification:** Pinpointing genuine, self-identified community requirements
* **Local Collaboration:** Partnering directly with local organisations and community leaders
* **Feasibility Assessment:** Evaluating sustainability, scope, and impact potential
* **Shared Agreement:** Establishing clear project goals, roles, and mutual responsibilities

---

## Project Areas

### 1. Education & Youth Development
* **Learning Support:** Tutoring and educational assistance
* **Digital Literacy:** Practical technology and computer skills
* **Career Mentorship:** Guidance for youth career pathways
* **Skill Development:** Vocational workshops and training
* **Health & Wellbeing:** Community health awareness and wellness programs

### 2. Research & Documentation (Women Empowerment & Livelihood)
* **Supporting Local Enterprises:** Assisting women-led initiatives and small businesses
* **Skill Enhancement:** Providing practical craft and business skills
* **Market Linkage:** Connecting local producers with broader markets and networks

### 3. Environment & Heritage
* **Conservation Activities:** Local environmental and ecological preservation projects
* **Community Awareness:** Promoting eco-friendly practices and sustainability
* **Cultural Documentation:** Documenting local traditions, oral histories, and heritage

---

## Implementation Model

Our structured four-phase model ensures effective, accountable, and sustainable outcomes:

### Phase 1: Understanding
* Initial community visits and context gathering
* In-depth needs assessment
* Direct consultations with local stakeholders

### Phase 2: Planning
* Defining clear, actionable objectives
* Preparing activity schedules and logistics
* Estimating resource requirements and project timelines

### Phase 3: Implementation
* Executing on-the-ground activities
* Actively engaging participants and local partners
* Monitoring ongoing progress and adaptation

### Phase 4: Evaluation
* Documenting measurable outcomes and community impact
* Gathering structured feedback from participants and partners
* Preparing transparent impact reports

## PRECAN Health Impact Projects

Through **Leave a Mark**, travellers and professionals have the opportunity to engage with meaningful public health initiatives in Nepal alongside **PRECAN (Prevent Cancer Nepal)**. These projects focus on cancer prevention, health awareness, early detection, and strengthening community-based health initiatives.

By combining local expertise, professional knowledge, and community participation, the aim is to create sustainable health impact beyond short-term activities. Participants can contribute through awareness programs, research support, community engagement, documentation, and skill-sharing while gaining a deeper understanding of Nepal’s public health challenges and solutions.

For more information, visit [Prevent Cancer Nepal (PRECAN)](https://www.precan.org/).
